# ACC-01 Account Structure
## 12 Risk and Control Map (v0.3)

| # | Risk | Consequence | Control | Evidence / test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subaccount owned by a different legal entity than its master | Cross-client money mixing; safeguarding evidence corrupted | Composite FK `(master_account_id, client_id)`; owner derived by trigger, never supplied | ACC1-T-004/005 |
| 2 | Account ownership silently changed | Client-money attribution and audit trail rewritten | Immutable by column grants + trigger; no transfer path designed; attempt audited Critical | T-006…010 |
| 3 | `client_id` mistaken for `subaccount_id` (or vice versa) downstream | Wrong-owner posting, limit or permission scope | Distinct opaque prefixed identifiers (`mac_`, `sac_`); resolve returns all three dimensions; DEC-011 §4.10 dimension rule in LED-01 DCR | T-001/002; DCR-ACC-LED-01 |
| 4 | Stale `active` account used after freeze or client suspension | Trading/withdrawal on a restricted account | Effective status computed live from CLT-01 + restrictions; **no cache**; consumers store `versions` evidence | T-039/045 |
| 5 | CLT-01 or ACC-01 unavailable read as "fine" | Fail-open on the hot path | `unknown` and 503 are both deny; never empty-200 | T-039/096 |
| 6 | Account created for an ineligible client (retail, pending, closed) | Regulatory breach; retail lock bypass | Client status **and** class checked at submit and again at apply through CLT-01 | T-043/054 |
| 7 | Unapproved, self-approved or **un-entitled** account change | Fraudulent account creation / restriction lifting | IAM-02 request/apply with payload-fingerprint-bound verify and single-use token; no direct create/set-status route. **Current IAM-02 does NOT entitlement-check makers or checkers (approval_required short-circuits before role lookup; approval routes unguarded — `IAM2-FIND-002`) and does NOT prove the authenticated apply actor is the maker (`actor_id` is a body field; `approval_id` unverified). ACC-01 provides neither and gates real governed apply on `DEP-IAM-ENTITLEMENT` and `DEP-IAM-ACTOR-BINDING`** (DCR-ACC-IAM-03/-04/-06); it consumes attested facts only and never fakes the proof | T-047…052, T-060, T-066, T-121, T-127…130, T-180…185, T-189 |
| 8 | Failure between token consumption and mutation | Consumed approval, unapplied change | Mirrors CLT-01/CFG-01: verify **before** the local transaction; any later failure rolls back and leaves the request `requested`; a **fresh** approval is required (no invented recovery state) | T-055…057 |
| 9 | Status changed without record | Unauditable freeze/unfreeze | Transition + history triggers; commit fails without cause; audit reference `NOT NULL` | T-027/028/088 |
| 10 | Restriction lapses or is lifted improperly | Frozen account released without authority | Authority-sourced restrictions cannot expire; lift needs evidence + approval; child recomputed, never blindly `active` | T-070…072 |
| 11 | Closure strands or mis-closes client money; **attest-then-post race**; **funded account sealed and trapped** (R2-F01) | Balance, open trade or settlement lost or unreachable; account closed with money | **Preventive, completable** closure: `closing` permits only the **closure-drain allow-list**; **pre-seal readiness** prevents sealing a funded account; the **final checker approval** precedes the seal; `closure_barrier` (LED-01 refuses every new posting when true) → **post-barrier** attestation with seal version, watermarks, max resolution version, in-flight status, **latest row only** → machine-verified CAS `closed`; **governed abort** for a closure that cannot complete; fail closed on unreachable/unconfigured/stale/mismatched; children (default included) before master | T-077…084, T-135…139, T-158…179 |
| 12 | Permission read as capability activation | A held permission or an `active` account treated as production approval | ACC-01 exposes status only; no `enabled` field; DEC-013/014 conjunction; denial attribution | T-061/062/065/113/114 |
| 13 | Subaccount purpose read as product eligibility | `payments`/`rwa` label taken as AIX Pay/RWA approval | Purpose is a label; never read by any control; CFG-01 gates products | T-065; blueprint §11.3 |
| 14 | Scoped permission widens access | Cross-subaccount access | Registry provides validation only; **IAM-02** enforces narrowing-only (DCR-ACC-IAM-01) | IAM-02 suite, §29 item 39 |
| 15 | Enforcement dependency ignored | Scope model shipped with zero effective permissions (IAM2-FIND-002) | Explicit environment-agnostic prerequisites (`DEP-IAM-ENTITLEMENT`, `DEP-PUBLIC-PERIMETER`). **Empty `role_permission` does NOT deny approval-gated actions under current IAM-02** — known-gap tests (T-048, T-060, T-066, T-127, T-128) document the real behaviour | T-048, T-060, T-066, T-127, T-128 |
| 16 | Existence oracle on client routes | Enumeration of accounts/clients | Observationally identical "no authority" responses | T-064 |
| 17 | Internal seam abuse (e.g. a compromised consumer) | Account enumeration, DoS | Per-module capability secrets, read-only seams, caller audit, rate alarms; interim shared-secret posture accepted (as FINDING-C) — **not** true per-caller cryptographic identity | T-091/094 |
| 18 | Public exposure before perimeter control | Pre-auth abuse (`FND-FIND-001`, HIGH, OPEN) | Client routes deferred; no internet exposure until the finding is resolved | Go-live gate |
| 19 | Route path trips or evades the licence-lock boot guard | Service fails to boot, or an Exchange-shaped route ships | Path allow-list source test | T-099 |
| 20 | Concurrent creation exceeds limit / two defaults | Policy breach, ambiguous default | Advisory lock; partial unique index | T-019/020/118 |
| 21 | Structure drifts from ledger/CLT-01 (no cross-schema FK) | Orphaned ledger accounts; accounts under closed clients | Structural reconciliation R-1…R-8 | file 13 |
| 22 | Subaccount segregation misread as client-money safeguarding | Regulatory misstatement (`A2-Q2`) | ACC-01 asserts **no** safeguarding property; open questions gate go-live for client money | file 15; file 14 |
| 23 | Test-harness fragility (shared DB, unscoped deletes, superuser runs) | False greens | Fail-loud canary, ownership-scoped fixtures, non-superuser role canary, no head pin | T-105…110 |
| 24 | ACC-01 built before its consumers agree the contract | Rework across LED-01/CLT-01/IAM-02 | DCR register (file 17); LED-01 may not freeze until it consumes DEC-011 **and** this pack | file 17 |
| 25 | Wrongly-created account cannot be closed until LED-01 attester exists | Stuck `closing` row consumes limits; blocks client closure | **No void/bypass.** Creation requires `DEP-LED-CLOSURE-CONTRACT` (environment-agnostic) until the attester exists; test-data reset is outside ACC-01; `closing`/`closure_sealed` still count against limits; ownership immutability and empty-attester fail-closed unchanged; a master can now complete closure (R2-F02) and a stuck closure has a governed abort | T-133, T-134, T-160, T-179 |
| 26 | `blocked_scopes` read as an exhaustive allow/deny list | Unlisted transaction-producing activity (settlement, Exchange access…) allowed for an `active_limited`/restricted account | **Consumer rule:** `effective_status ≠ active` ⇒ deny unless explicitly authorised; scopes explanatory only; `active_limited`/`restricted` ⇒ report-only; no permit field in resolve | T-036/037/041/140/141 |
| 27 | Account module holds a general write-capable credential of CLT-01 or IAM-02 — **including "temporarily" in DEV/TEST** | Identity/membership mutation reach; approval forgery | Dedicated read/verify-scoped credentials or labelled test doubles **in every environment** (DCR-ACC-CLT-03, DCR-ACC-IAM-05; ACC-R2-HD-08); every CLT-01 read (incl. apply-time) uses the read-scoped client; source guard; boot check across all environments | T-146, T-186…188 |
| 28 | Restriction pairs one client's master with another client's subaccount | Cross-client restriction applied | Composite FK (subaccount, master, client) | T-145 |
| 29 | Late housekeeping job leaves a restriction unenforced | Fail-open window | Time-effective resolution; job is housekeeping only; `applied_restriction_ids` evidence | T-142…144 |
| 30 | Default subaccount used as fallback target | Re-creates the `client_id` conflation; implicit product authorisation | Structural-only; explicit `subaccount_id` required; no default lookup; `is_default` not exposed | T-122…125 |
| 31 | Two freeze systems (CLT-01 client freeze vs ACC-01 restriction) diverge | Inconsistent lift paths; ACC-01 mistaken for client freeze | ACC-01 restriction never substitutes for a client freeze; ownership ungoverned ⇒ `DEP-FREEZE-GOVERNANCE` gates restriction operations (DCR-ACC-GOV-05) | T-151, T-152 |
| 32 | Readiness couples services (boot-order cycle) | Peer outage prevents boot / deadlock | Readiness = configuration + contract only; no outbound peer call | T-132 |
| 33 | Invented retention period conflicts with platform policy | Records destroyed or over-retained | No hard delete; no ACC-01 retention period; platform/client-record policy once defined (DCR-ACC-GOV-06) | T-147 |
| 34 | **Closure barrier masked by a more severe descriptive status** (sealed + suspended/frozen/restricted) (R2-F03) | A future status→activity policy authorises an activity through the seal; posting after the barrier | `closure_barrier` is an independent stored fact returned by resolve; consumers evaluate it **first**; conjunctive component check; `effective_status` descriptive only; LED-01 refuses "when `closure_barrier = true`" | T-153…157 |
| 35 | Master and default subaccount block each other's closure (R2-F02) | No master can ever close; every client holding an account un-closable | Master + default enter `closing` atomically; every child closes first; deferred family trigger; `FOR SHARE` master read on child creation prevents a child appearing mid-closure | T-031, T-160…165 |
| 36 | Posting resolved before the seal commits after it, undetected (R2-F01) | Money moves on a closed/closing-out account | Version/watermark evidence: `committed_after_preseal_watermark = 0`, `max_resolution_version_committed < closure_sealed_at_version`, in-flight fence; **latest attestation only**; no wall-clock primary control | T-170…173 |
| 37 | Governed abort abused as an operational shortcut or "reopen" | Closure requirements bypassed; barrier lifted improperly; a denied status restored | Maker-checker only; evidence-conditioned (`ACC1_CLOSURE_ABORT_INVALID` without blocking evidence); clears the barrier only inside the recovery transaction; restores only the recomputed projection; Critical audit; no other route | T-174…179 |
| 38 | ACC-01 keeps a parallel environment-availability control (R2-F08) | Two authorities for `ENVIRONMENT_AVAILABILITY`; ACC-01 code change lifts a gate | **No environment logic in ACC-01**; environment-agnostic `DEP-*` prerequisites only; CFG-01 owns availability (DCR-ACC-CFG-02); test doubles injected at test composition roots only | T-121, T-188…191 |
| 39 | Restriction cannot be lifted/cancelled because housekeeping has not run, or a future restriction cannot be withdrawn (R2-F07) | Availability defect; stuck restriction | Legality decided by effective time; inline activation inside lift; governed `cancel_scheduled_restriction` | T-192…198 |
