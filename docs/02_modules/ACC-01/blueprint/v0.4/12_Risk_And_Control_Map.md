# ACC-01 Account Structure
## 12 Risk and Control Map (v0.4)

| # | Risk | Consequence | Control | Evidence / test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subaccount owned by a different legal entity than its master | Cross-client money mixing; safeguarding evidence corrupted | Composite FK `(master_account_id, client_id)`; owner derived by trigger, never supplied | ACC1-T-004/005 |
| 2 | Account ownership silently changed | Client-money attribution and audit trail rewritten | Immutable by column grants + trigger; no transfer path designed; attempt audited Critical | T-006…010 |
| 3 | `client_id` mistaken for `subaccount_id` (or vice versa) downstream | Wrong-owner posting, limit or permission scope | Distinct opaque prefixed identifiers (`mac_`, `sac_`); resolve returns all three dimensions; DEC-011 §4.10 dimension rule in LED-01 DCR | T-001/002; DCR-ACC-LED-01 |

| 6 | Account created for an ineligible client (retail, pending, closed) | Regulatory breach; retail lock bypass | Client status **and** class checked at submit and again at apply through CLT-01 | T-043/054 |
| 7 | Unapproved, self-approved or **un-entitled** account change | Fraudulent account creation / restriction lifting | IAM-02 request/apply with payload-fingerprint-bound verify and single-use token; no direct create/set-status route. **Current IAM-02 does NOT entitlement-check makers or checkers (approval_required short-circuits before role lookup; approval routes unguarded — `IAM2-FIND-002`) and does NOT prove the authenticated apply actor is the maker (`actor_id` is a body field; `approval_id` unverified). | 8 | Failure between token consumption and mutation | Consumed approval, unapplied change | Mirrors CLT-01/CFG-01: verify **before** the local transaction; any later failure rolls back and leaves the request `requested`; a **fresh** approval is required (no invented recovery state) | T-055…057 |
| 9 | Status changed without record | Unauditable freeze/unfreeze | Transition + history triggers; commit fails without cause; audit reference `NOT NULL` | T-027/028/088 |

| 12 | Permission read as capability activation | A held permission or an `active` account treated as production approval | ACC-01 exposes status only; no `enabled` field; DEC-013/014 conjunction; denial attribution | T-061/062/065/113/114 |
| 13 | Subaccount purpose read as product eligibility | `payments`/`rwa` label taken as AIX Pay/RWA approval | Purpose is a label; never read by any control; CFG-01 gates products | T-065; blueprint §11.3 |
| 14 | Scoped permission widens access | Cross-subaccount access | Registry provides validation only; **IAM-02** enforces narrowing-only (DCR-ACC-IAM-01) | IAM-02 suite, §29 item 39 |

| 16 | Existence oracle on client routes | Enumeration of accounts/clients | Observationally identical "no authority" responses | T-064 |

| 26 | `blocked_scopes` read as an exhaustive allow/deny list | Unlisted transaction-producing activity (settlement, Exchange access…) allowed for an `active_limited`/restricted account | **Consumer rule:** `effective_status ≠ active` ⇒ deny unless explicitly authorised; scopes explanatory only; `active_limited`/`restricted` ⇒ report-only; no permit field in resolve | T-036/037/041/140/141 |
| 27 | Account module holds a general write-capable credential of CLT-01 or IAM-02 — **including "temporarily" in DEV/TEST** | Identity/membership mutation reach; approval forgery | Dedicated read/verify-scoped credentials or labelled test doubles **in every environment** (DCR-ACC-CLT-03, DCR-ACC-IAM-05; ACC-R2-HD-08); every CLT-01 read (incl. apply-time) uses the read-scoped client; source guard; boot check across all environments | T-146, T-186…188, T-227, T-228, T-230 |

| 32 | Readiness couples services (boot-order cycle) | Peer outage prevents boot / deadlock | Readiness = configuration + contract only; no outbound peer call | T-132 |
| 33 | Invented retention period conflicts with platform policy | Records destroyed or over-retained | No hard delete; no ACC-01 retention period; platform/client-record policy once defined (DCR-ACC-GOV-06) | T-147 |

| 35 | Master and default subaccount block each other's closure (R2-F02) | No master can ever close; every client holding an account un-closable | **(R3)** Master + default + master-directed children enter `closing` atomically; children seal and **wait**; the master seals; **one atomic transaction** closes the family; deferred family trigger; `FOR SHARE` master read on child creation prevents a child appearing mid-closure | T-031, T-160…165, T-199…212 |
| 36 | Posting resolved before the seal commits after it, undetected (R2-F01) | Money moves on a closed/closing-out account | Version/watermark evidence: `committed_after_preseal_watermark = 0` against the **pinned** W_pre, `max_resolution_version_committed < closure_sealed_at_version`, in-flight fence; **commit-ordered** watermark required of LED-01; **latest attestation only**, bound to the seal pin; no wall-clock primary control | T-170…173, T-213…222 |
| 37 | Governed abort abused as an operational shortcut or "reopen" | Closure requirements bypassed; barrier lifted improperly; a denied status restored | Maker-checker only; evidence-conditioned (`ACC1_CLOSURE_ABORT_INVALID` without blocking evidence; for a master, evidence of a family member only); clears the barrier only inside the recovery transaction; restores only the recomputed projection; Critical audit; no other route. **Stated honestly:** in `closing` any undrained target is sufficient evidence, so the control is maker-checker + recorded evidence + audit | T-174…179, T-201, T-239 |
| 38 | ACC-01 keeps a parallel environment-availability control (R2-F08) | Two authorities for `ENVIRONMENT_AVAILABILITY`; ACC-01 code change lifts a gate | **No environment logic in ACC-01**; environment-agnostic `DEP-*` prerequisites only; CFG-01 owns availability (DCR-ACC-CFG-02); test doubles injected at test composition roots only | T-121, T-188…191, T-223, T-224 |
| 39 | Restriction cannot be lifted/cancelled because housekeeping has not run, or a future restriction cannot be withdrawn (R2-F07) | Availability defect; stuck restriction | Legality decided by effective time; inline activation inside lift; governed `cancel_scheduled_restriction` | T-192…198 |
| 40 | **(R3-F01)** A master abort (or any master-level failure) after its children closed leaves an **ACTIVE master with a permanently `closed` default** — no path restores a default; the next master closure is undefined; the client is un-closable | Structural invariant broken; account structure loses its subaccount dimension | **A master-directed child never becomes `closed` on its own** (ACC-R3-HD-01): children stop at `closure_sealed`; the whole family closes in one atomic transaction or not at all; a family abort reverses master + default + children; deferred `trg_acc1_master_default_invariant` makes the state unrepresentable; reconciliation R-8 | T-199…T-212 |
| 41 | **(R3-F01, R3-F05)** An independently initiated child closure is silently adopted, completed or reversed by a master closure or abort | A child's own approved closure is reversed without its own evidence, or completed without its own approval | Explicit membership (`closure_family_member`, `independent_preserved`, its own initiation id); independent closures are never adopted, never reversed by a master abort, and block the master seal until they are `closed` (family eligibility rule); they have their own abort with their own evidence | T-202, T-203, T-238 |
| 42 | **(R3-F02)** The readiness the checker approved is not the readiness completion relies on ("latest readiness" changes after the seal) | A post-seal posting is closed over; the seal-to-completion race defence is compared to the wrong watermark | The seal request **pins** readiness id/seq/W_pre/payload hash/cycle/target version/family set hash; seal apply persists an immutable **seal pin**; readiness inserts are refused once the target leaves `closing`; completion and attestation compare against the pin | T-213…T-222 |
| 43 | **(R3-F03)** A dependency prerequisite is satisfied by self-declared configuration (a version string, a governance reference, a peer token that merely works) | The only runtime interlock on ACC-01's governed operations is a configuration edit | ACC-R3-HD-03: a `DEP-*` is satisfied only by behaviour verified from the owning service per operation or is **hard-unsatisfied** (governance-only); no config key can declare evidence; unknown/missing evidence refuses; no fake boot detection of a secret ACC-01 cannot know | T-223…T-232 |
| 44 | **(R3-F04)** The "authenticated actor assertion" is produced by ACC-01 itself and echoed back as "attested" | Caller-asserted actor posture presented as proof | IAM-02 verifies the actor independently (IAM-01 session/recent-auth/assertion via its own trusted seam); an ACC-01-minted assertion never satisfies `DEP-IAM-ACTOR-BINDING`; source guard: ACC-01 never mints or signs an identity assertion | T-233…T-235 |
| 45 | **(R3-F05)** Governed abort is unavailable exactly when the LED-01 attester or contract is what failed | A barred target stays barred until configuration is restored | Abort needs the governed-apply set only (no `DEP-LED-CLOSURE-CONTRACT`), makes no LED-01 call and uses ACC-01's own evidence and local family state | T-236, T-237 |
| 46 | **(R3-F06)** Maker-only closure initiation lets one entitled maker halt client activity (`closing`), a de facto single-actor restriction (interaction with pending HD-4) | Operational disruption or abuse by a single actor | Entitlement-checked non-approval permission (never an approval-gated code); attested initiator entitlement; Critical audit that fails closed; `closing` cannot seal without the checker's final approval; maker-checker governed abort undoes it; HD-4 remains an open human decision | T-240…T-244 |
| 47 | **(R3-F07)** A restriction cancelled in a time-of-check window (`now()` = transaction start) after resolves counted it in force; application-clock skew changes legality | Cancel without lift evidence; inconsistent enforcement | Database `clock_timestamp()` in the locked check plus a deferred commit-time check; application clock never read; residual commit-latency window bounded and evidenced by `applied_restriction_ids` and the version bump | T-246, T-247 |
