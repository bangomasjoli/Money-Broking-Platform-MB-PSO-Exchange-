## 3. Regulatory Support

KYC-01 supports:

1. Customer due diligence evidence.
2. Corporate/institutional KYB evidence.
3. Beneficial ownership/control verification.
4. Authorised-party verification.
5. Periodic review/perpetual KYC.
6. Enhanced due diligence routing.
7. Auditable CDD outcome publication to CLT-01.
8. Evidence for audit/regulatory review.
9. Data protection and sensitive access controls.
